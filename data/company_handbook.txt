Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Section 1: Company Overview
Acme Corp was founded in 2010 and specializes in cloud-based logistics software. The company is headquartered in Austin, Texas, and has over 500 employees across 12 countries. Acme's mission is to make global supply chains more transparent and efficient.

Section 2: Working Hours
Standard working hours at Acme Corp are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees are entitled to a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Flexible working arrangements can be requested through the HR portal and are typically approved within 5 business days.

Section 3: Vacation Policy
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid vacation per year during their first two years of employment. After two years of continuous service, the accrual rate increases to 22 days per year. Unused vacation days can be carried over into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

Section 4: Remote Work Policy
Acme Corp operates on a hybrid work model. Employees are expected to be in the office at least 3 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday being mandatory in-office days. Fully remote arrangements may be approved for employees who relocate more than 100 miles from the nearest office, subject to manager approval.

Section 5: Health Benefits
All full-time employees are eligible for health insurance starting on their first day of employment. Acme Corp covers 90% of the premium for the employee and 60% of the premium for dependents. Dental and vision coverage are also included at no additional cost to the employee.

Section 6: Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in June and December. Reviews are based on a combination of self-assessment, peer feedback, and manager evaluation. Salary adjustments and promotions are typically decided following the December review cycle.

Section 7: Expense Reimbursement
Employees can submit expense reports through the internal Concur system. Reimbursable expenses include client meals (up to $75 per person), travel costs for approved business trips, and home office equipment (up to $500 per year). Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
